Our Story

The genesis of MSC Legal can be traced back to 1960, the year when it first started as an office under the name Manbir Singh & Company. Through years of serving clients with excellent legal services, MSC Legal has evolved into a multi-disciplinary law office offering myriad legal solutions across a vast variety of fields.

Our services are a result of ritualistic adherence to our core values and principles — efficiency, excellence, and reliability. These have allowed us to develop and maintain a client-centric framework which caters to all kinds of needs with care and attention.

We take pride in having evolved into a firm which takes a systematic and pragmatic approach to all problems and queries; a unique solution for every situation crafted with artful creativity and framed with success in mind.
